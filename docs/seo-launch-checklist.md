# SEO launch checklist

Use this checklist after SEO changes reach production.

## Search Console

- Confirm the production property is verified.
- Submit `https://yaqoob-enterprises.vercel.app/sitemap.xml` once.
- Inspect the homepage and the priority biometric, printing, passport-photo and online-form URLs.
- Request indexing after the title and redirect changes deploy; do not repeatedly resubmit unchanged URLs.
- Check Page indexing, HTTPS, Core Web Vitals and Enhancements weekly for the first month.
- Record queries, impressions, clicks and average position every 28 days.

## Google Business Profile

Official profile: https://share.google/2S03TGi4gfULWqI6F

- Keep the real-world business name exactly `Yaqoob Enterprises`; do not add ranking keywords to the name.
- Confirm one consistent address, phone number, website, opening hours and map location.
- Verify the primary category against the service that customers most often visit for.
- Add only services that are genuinely available.
- Upload current storefront, signboard, counter, equipment and service photos.
- Ask real customers for honest reviews without incentives or review gating.
- Reply to every review without copying the same response.

## Facebook and Instagram

Official profiles:

- https://www.facebook.com/yaqoobenterprises1
- https://www.instagram.com/yaqoobenterprises1/

Use the same business name, phone, address, opening hours, website and WhatsApp number in both profiles. Publish real service updates and shop photos instead of generic quote posts.

## URL changes

- Keep the Vercel URL canonical until a custom domain is purchased.
- When a custom domain is introduced, map every old URL to its matching new URL with a permanent redirect.
- Update Search Console, Google Business Profile, social profiles, structured data and citations in the same migration.
- Keep redirects active for at least one year.

## Monthly review

- Compare Search Console landing pages and queries.
- Check Google Business calls, website clicks and direction requests.
- Check review count, rating and unanswered reviews.
- Fix broken links, unexpected redirects, duplicate titles and sitemap errors before publishing more pages.
